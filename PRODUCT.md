# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Joe Pierce for software engineering roles. They need to understand who Joe is, what he can do, and why his background is distinctive within a few seconds.

## Product Purpose

The site is Joe Pierce's professional CV and portfolio surface. Its purpose is to make his experience and skill set immediately clear and visually engaging so recruiters and hiring managers consider him for a software role and contact him.

Success means a visitor can rapidly identify Joe as an expert Python and software engineer, understand the relevance of his specialist background, and confidently take the next step of contacting him.

## Positioning

Joe combines expert Python skills and substantial professional software engineering experience with an uncommon background in hydroinformatics, hydrology, and scientific computing. The site should make this combination legible as a coherent professional advantage rather than presenting the domains as unrelated experience.

## Operating Context

The primary experience is a single-page web CV designed for rapid scanning. The same content must also produce a polished, visually appealing, print-ready one-page CV, although visitors are not expected to print it themselves as their main action.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing copy, facts, employment history, terminology, and contact details exactly. Future design work must not rewrite, shorten, embellish, or replace them.
- Preserve the single-page printable behaviour and one-page print output.
- Future work should focus on style and layout rather than content changes.
- The implementation is a static Jekyll site published through GitHub Pages.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the name Joe Pierce and the professional identity expressed by the existing CV content.
- Prioritise clarity and visual engagement, with the essential identity and skill set understandable within a few seconds.

## Evidence on Hand

- The complete approved CV copy, employment history, education, professional membership, skills, and contact details are in `index.md`.
- The current web structure is in `_layouts/default.html` and `assets/css/style.scss`.
- A dedicated A4 print implementation is in `assets/css/print.css`; it is evidence of the required one-page printable behaviour and must not be lost.
- No additional testimonials, client endorsements, performance claims, or other proof are currently provided; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Make Joe's professional identity and strongest capabilities obvious within seconds.
2. Present software engineering expertise and scientific-domain depth as one differentiated professional story.
3. Improve visual engagement through style and layout while preserving every approved word and fact.
4. Treat the polished single-page print version as a first-class output, not an afterthought.
